# v1.1.0

## NEW

- copycats on contraptions and trains are drawn in the material they are skinned in, with the right shape. before, create's copycat panels and steps did not draw at all.
- copycats+ copycats are drawn too if you have it installed: slabs, stairs, beams, boards, bytes, layers and the rest. blocks with several materials show each part in its own material.
- an unskinned copycat draws with the copycat frame texture, like it does in game.
- copycats on sable ships are drawn as well when this addon is installed alongside bluemap: aeronautics.

## CHANGED

- carriages are meshed again once after updating, so existing trains pick up their copycats.
